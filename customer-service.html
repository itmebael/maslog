Common Questions

How do I cancel a booking?

Contact customer service at least 24 hours before your booking date. Cancellations are subject to our refund policy.

What if I miss my booking date?

10% of your payment will not be refunded if you fail to arrive on your confirmed date without prior notice.

How long does approval take?

Online bookings are reviewed by staff within 24 hours. Check My Bookings for status updates.
